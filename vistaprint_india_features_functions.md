# VistaPrint India --- Website Features & Functions

**Reference:** VistaPrint India (`vistaprint.in`)\
**Reviewed:** September 2026

## 1. Website Overview

VistaPrint India is an online custom-printing and personalization
platform. Users can select a physical product, customize it with
templates or their own design, configure product options, add it to a
cart, and place an order.

The platform covers business printing, marketing materials, stationery,
packaging, apparel, gifts, photo products, promotional products, and
related design tools.

## 4. Online Design & Customization

One of the core functions is online product customization.

### Template-based design

Users can:

1.  Select a product.
2.  Select product specifications.
3.  Browse pre-designed templates.
4.  Choose a template.
5.  Replace/customize text and other supported elements.
6.  Preview the design.
7.  Add the finished product to the cart.

VistaPrint provides large template libraries with filters. For standard
visiting cards, the template page currently shows thousands of templates
and filters such as orientation, corners, stock, finish, industry,
style/theme, logo/photo area, colour, and use case.

### Upload existing design

Users can also upload a completed design instead of starting from a
template.

Supported file types vary by product. VistaPrint's flyer page, for
example, lists:

-   PDF
-   AI
-   JPEG
-   PNG
-   DOC
-   PPT

The uploaded design must follow the product's dimensions and
print-safety requirements.

## 14. Delivery Functions

VistaPrint India provides different delivery options depending on
product and location.

The website currently advertises **Same Day Delivery** for eligible pin
codes in:

-   Mumbai
-   Bengaluru
-   Kolkata

Standard delivery is also available.

Delivery eligibility and available speeds depend on the product and
destination.

## 15. Cash on Delivery

Cash on Delivery is supported for eligible orders/products and delivery
methods.

The website currently states that COD is not available for Same Day
Delivery orders.

## 27. Core Business Model

The website follows a **customized print-on-demand / e-commerce model**:

``` text
Customer
   ↓
Select Product
   ↓
Customize Product
   ↓
Configure Quantity & Options
   ↓
Place Order
   ↓
VistaPrint Production
   ↓
Shipping
   ↓
Customer
```

The platform combines an online design experience with physical product
manufacturing and delivery.
